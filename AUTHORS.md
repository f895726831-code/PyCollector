## Acknowledgements

PyCollector was extracted from a production insurance data collection platform that
manages 48+ company data sources across 5 data domains. The framework represents the
generic, reusable infrastructure that emerged from that project.

Special thanks to the original project team for their contributions to the patterns
and practices that inspired this framework.
